# Targeted-RPT figure caption

Fig. | Incremental testing burdens with targeted deployment RPT. a, Proposed diagnoses all deployment cells, (1-p)N, during required de-energisation. After that discharge, q(1-p)N selected cells receive a full RPT from zero SOC: 1.5 h charge + 10 min rest + r h discharge, with E_cell/eta of extra purchased electricity per selected cell. The common initial-SOC-to-zero discharge baseline is subtracted once. The training fraction p sets the labelling pool; q sets additional deployment retesting. High uncertainty and high estimated capacity describe the selection policy, not a validated risk distribution. Selected cells are modelled using the batch representative retention; no accuracy improvement is established here. b, Incremental cost half-violins from the supplied 800,000-draw primary-seed density cache per method, using six rows: Proposed (no targeted RPT), Proposed+targeted RPT, Pulse-based, EIS-based, Partial-charge-based and RPT-based. Ordinary Proposed uses the labelling-stage costs from the same draws (q=0), checked against the supplied q=0 cost summary; it is a panel-b reference, not an additional comparator in panels c-l. The two Proposed scenarios share a rose colour family, with the non-targeted scenario lighter. Densities use 160 shared equal-width log10-cost bins and one-bin Gaussian smoothing (reflect boundary, four-sigma truncation); widths are normalized separately. Black points and intervals show medians and 2.5th-97.5th percentiles. The logarithmic x axis has minor ticks at 2-9 per decade. c, Median paired percentage reductions relative to each comparator. d-f, Paired cost, purchased-electricity and CO2 savings, shown as medians and central 95% simulation intervals, not confidence intervals. Absolute quantities are per tonne; positive savings favour Proposed, whereas negative savings indicate an increase in the corresponding burden. Simulation intervals can cross zero; negative intervals are fully displayed and dashed lines mark zero savings. Relative to Pulse-based, purchased-electricity savings are negative in 9.54% of primary draws. Relative to Pulse-based, CO2 savings are negative in 9.54% of primary draws. Panels b-f use seed 20260921 with 16 independent triangular inputs, including p ~ Tri(0.05, 0.15, 0.30), initial SOC ~ Tri(0.2, 0.4, 0.6), and q ~ Tri(0.03, 0.04, 0.05). The 15 shared inputs use seed 20260921 and q uses a separate independent stream. g-i, Deterministic one-factor cost sensitivity to retention, initial SOC and labelled fraction. Unscanned inputs stay at r=0.8, initial SOC=0.4 and p=0.15 as applicable; q=0.04 (4% of the deployment pool) is fixed throughout g-l. Average capacity retention (%) is 100r relative to nominal capacity. j-l, Continuous two-factor cost-reduction surfaces: labelled fraction and initial SOC at r=0.8, capacity and initial SOC at p=0.15, and labelled fraction and capacity at initial SOC=0.4. Each 2-by-2 group shows Pulse-based and EIS-based in the top row, and Partial-charge-based and RPT-based in the bottom row. Horizontal and vertical axes correspond to the first and second factors. All twelve maps use the current 51-by-51 grids and a common cividis colour scale from 25 to 95%. Displayed source bounds are 29.551718 to 90.328392%. The default 30-95% scale expands in five-point steps if current values require it; no source values are clipped. Contour labels give cost reduction in percent, with local interpolation between grid nodes and no surface fitting or smoothing. Proposed-versus-RPT responses are read from the current data and depend on initial SOC. Economic and equipment inputs remain fixed in g-l. These are protocol-conditioned model results, not industrial validation. Current source data accompany the panels.

Layout: panels j-l have row-major method ordering and automatic gaps around titles and ticks.
